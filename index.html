Supervisors

Supervisor registrations

Prospective supervisors fill in the registration form. Each form goes to the Course Coordinator / Research Officer of its school, then — only where an admin requires it — the Head of School for endorsement, then the Associate Dean Research / RSC for a decision. Approved supervisors join the supervisor pool; a sign-in account is created only when they are invited for a semester. Open a form to assign it to a specific person.

Registration form link

Public page — no sign-in needed. Send it to anyone who wants to register as a supervisor.

Reviewing officers

Who acts at each stage, and who the form is emailed to. Pick an administrator, a supervisor or a separate review-only account. Administrators can act at any stage regardless; naming one here makes them the person the form is routed to.

Loading…

Limits the Course Coordinator / Research Officer and Head of School stages to that school's forms (e.g. the Business course coordinator only gets Business forms). Leave it as Any for someone who handles every school, such as the Associate Dean Research.

Head of School endorsement

Optional, per school. When ticked, that school's forms go from the Course Coordinator / Research Officer to the Head of School for endorsement before the Associate Dean Research. When not ticked, they go straight to the Associate Dean Research / RSC.

Account policy

This app has no mail server, so nothing is emailed automatically. After each step you are offered a pre-filled email to the next office, and each officer sees their queue when they sign in.

Supporting documents *

The Research Office needs your CV, PhD certificate and academic transcripts, plus any other relevant documents. Choose how you will provide them.

Make sure anyone with the link can open your documents. In Google Drive:
  1. Put your documents in one folder (or upload each file).
  2. Right-click the folder or file → Share → Share.
  3. Under General access, change Restricted to Anyone with the link, with the role Viewer. Viewers can open and download the files.
  4. Click Copy link and paste it below.
Tip: open the link in a private / incognito window. If it asks you to sign in or request access, the sharing setting is not right yet.
…or a link to each document
